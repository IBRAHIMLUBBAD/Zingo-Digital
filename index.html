FAQ

Good to know.

Do I need an account to place an order?

Yes — an account keeps your files, order history and invoices in one dashboard, and lets you reorder a past service in a couple of clicks.

What happens after I upload a file?

It's stored securely and linked to your order number. You'll see it in your dashboard alongside the finished result once the order is complete.

Can I change what a dynamic QR code points to?

Yes — a dynamic QR code's destination can be updated any time without reprinting the code itself.

Is document/licensing work handled the same way?

Services involving official documents are only listed after we've confirmed the issuing authority, eligibility and legal requirements for that market.
